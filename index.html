Each figure shows MA-3 (correlation of the 3-year moving average, i.e. trend shape) with the raw year-to-year correlation beside it — the gap between them shows how much is method vs. data. MA is defensible here because single-year crowd counts carry large measurement error, so the trend, not the exact yearly value, is the object of interest. Pre-1989 West Berlin ran two separate May Day traditions: Rucht (DGB) — Dieter Rucht's dataset for the mainstream union march — and Linke — the alternative/left mobilisation (corrected series). In 1970, 1971 and 1977 the DGB held no street march (only a closed hall event, ~500), so the left carried the day; the two are stacked, not merged. East Berlin (FDGB) figures are official/order-of-magnitude. 1990–2019 comes live from the editor. Two sliders: top = start year, bottom = end year.
